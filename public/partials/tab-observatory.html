verified targets by bearing and range from us

Rings scale to the record, not a fixed range. A faded dot has been reached rarely — the dot says reached, the fade says how dependably. Distances beyond 200 km are excluded as unverifiable.

the mesh's own ground — every hop we have witnessed

A relay is drawn larger the more of our traffic it has carried. Line weight is how often that hop has been witnessed. Only the far clusters are named — one label per neighbourhood, with +n for the nodes underneath.

every packet heard on air, and where the antenna was pointing

A bearing is recorded only when a directional antenna on a settled rotator heard the packet — otherwise blank, never zero. Relayed packets are kept: the distant nodes worth locating are exactly the ones that arrive via relays.
